05 / Before we start

A few useful answers.

How much does a website cost?

The quote depends on the number of pages, design and features. Send a brief and I’ll discuss the scope with you before agreeing on a price.

How long will my project take?

Timing depends on the scope, content and integrations. We’ll agree on a timeline after discussing what you need.

Will the website work on phones?

Responsive layouts are part of my approach. The aim is a clear, usable experience across phones, tablets and desktops, with checks appropriate to the project.

What do I need to provide?

A description of your goals, your logo and brand assets if you have them, page content and examples of websites you like. We can discuss what is missing at the start.

How do payments and ongoing support work?

Payment stages, delivery scope and any maintenance arrangements are agreed before work begins. Hosting, domains and third-party services can carry separate costs.

Can you update a website I already have?

Yes. Share the website link and the changes you need, and I can review what is possible before we agree on the work.
